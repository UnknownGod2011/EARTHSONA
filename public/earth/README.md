# Earth basemap

`blue-marble-july-2004.webp` is a 2048 × 1024 WebP derivative of the July 2004 global Blue Marble Next Generation image with topographic and bathymetric shading. The 5400 × 2700 source JPEG was retrieved on 2026-11-14, verified with Pillow, converted to RGB, downsampled with Lanczos, and saved as WebP at quality 88. No crop, rotation, or geographic correction was applied. This is a fixed monthly composite for geographic context.

- Credit NASA Earth Observatory, Reto Stöckli, Eric Vermote, Nazmi Saleous, Robert Simmon and David Herring. [NASA Earth Observatory product and downloads](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/)
- The 2:1 WGS84 geographic raster spans 180° W to 180° E from left to right and 90° N to 90° S from top to bottom. [Blue Marble Next Generation technical documentation](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/content-feature/bluemarble/bmng.pdf)
- Reused with credit for education and information under NASA's media usage guidelines. Attribute NASA when reusing the imagery and avoid implying endorsement. [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
- [Official 5400 × 2700 source JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/july/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg)
